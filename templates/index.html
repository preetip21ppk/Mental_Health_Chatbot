What is Mental Health?

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act. It also helps determine how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices. Mental health is important at every stage of life, from childhood and adolescence through adulthood.

Mental Health Facts

  • Mental health is essential to overall health and well-being.
  • Mental illnesses are common, but help is available, and most people with mental illnesses recover.
  • Mental health disorders affect an estimated 26% of American adults each year.
  • Many factors contribute to mental health problems, including biological factors, such as genes or brain chemistry, and life experiences, such as trauma or abuse.
  • Mental health problems are not a sign of weakness and are treatable.
